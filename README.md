## The forest floor is a mess. Your finger is the broom.

**LeafSweeper** is a cozy, pressure-free puzzle game for Android phones
about tidying up nature's mess. Somewhere under the leaves, petals, sticks
and stones, a little critter is waiting. Sweep the litter away until you
spot it — then tap it to win the round.

There are no timers. No fail state. The bug never runs away.
It's just you, the leaves, and a very patient ladybug. 🐞

## How to play

1. **Sweep** — drag your finger across the screen and brush the debris
   away, a little at a time. Heavy rocks and sticks take more effort
   than a light dusting of petals.
2. **Look closer** — something is hiding under there. Don't stare for a
   color; look for a *shape*.
3. **Tap!** — petals fly, the sun beams, and the game tells you how you
   did ("Just 18 sweeps to find the bug!") — plus a real fun fact about
   the critter you just met.
4. **Next round** — the litter keeps growing, ever so gently, forever.

## A hidden world to collect

- **39 species** hide in the leaves — ladybugs, butterflies, snails,
  fireflies, dragonflies, stick insects, water striders and more.
- **156 looks to find** — four natural colors for every species, all
  logged in your **Bug Collection Book**: a flip-through book where bugs
  you haven't met yet wait as little misty silhouettes, and each find is
  counted as you go.
- **1 in 100 rounds hides something special** — a ✨ **prismatic** bug
  wearing a shifting rainbow sheen. Find it and it rides a sun flare up
  to a grandiose near-white win card while the circling litter turns to
  a swirl of gold and white.

## And then the weather turns 🌧️

Every 10th level brings a **storm**: dark skies, drifting fog,
lightning — and gusts of wind that blow fresh leaves right back onto the
ground you just swept. Remembering where you've already cleaned becomes
your broom. (Don't worry — there's still no way to lose.)

## Made to be kind

- **No timers, no lives, no losing.** Play for two minutes or two hours.
- **Difficulty grows with you**, slowly and politely.
- **Your progress stays on your own phone.** No accounts, no sign-ups.
- The game remembers your favorite critter and your best rounds — and
  it likes to chat about them between levels.
