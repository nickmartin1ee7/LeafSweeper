LeafSweeper is a cozy, pressure-free puzzle game for Android phones. A
little critter is hiding under the leaves, petals, sticks and stones on
a patch of forest floor. Sweep the litter away until you spot it, then
tap it to win the round. There are no timers and no fail state — the
bug never runs away.

## How to play

1. **Sweep** — drag your finger across the screen to brush debris away,
   a little at a time. Heavy rocks and sticks take more effort than
   light petals.
2. **Tap the bug** when you spot it. Look for shapes, not colors — the
   game tells you how you did and shares a real fun fact about the
   species you found.
3. **Next round** — the litter grows slowly, forever.

## Features

- **39 bug species** with four natural color variants each (156 looks
  to find), logged in a flip-through Bug Collection Book.
- **Rare prismatic bugs** — 1 round in 100 hides a rainbow-sheened
  critter with an extra celebration when you find it.
- **Seasons** — the year turns as you clear levels: Summer storms
  spawn a tornado that shuffles the floor, Fall washes everything
  downstream, and Winter buries the bug in ice — crack it out with the
  hammer you dig up. Finish the year at level 400 for permanent
  bonuses.
- **Storm levels** — every 10th level the sky darkens and the wind
  blows fresh litter back onto ground you already swept, so memory
  becomes the challenge.
- **Local saves** — your progress stays on your phone. No accounts.
